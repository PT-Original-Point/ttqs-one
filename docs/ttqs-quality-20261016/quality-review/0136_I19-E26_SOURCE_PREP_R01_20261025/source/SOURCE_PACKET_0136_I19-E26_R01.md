## Evidence boundary

The register tracks three separate evidence classes:

1. Social-benefit activity: source-backed organizer, date, the association's documented role, delivery, and separately supported social outcome.
2. Public/private award: issuing body, award title/category/date, recipient as shown by the original, and a verifiable issuer record.
3. School partnership / industry-academia / R&D social impact: school/unit, project or agreement, the association's actual contribution, deliverable, acceptance/public output, and evidence linking it to a social result.

These are source-listed reference evidence types; one record must not fabricate all three. An MOU alone does not prove an outcome. Participation alone does not prove social impact. A press report is routed to DOCX 0137 / I19-E27 and cannot replace its underlying original. Market/customer-value trends belong to DOCX 0135 / I19-E25 and cannot be imported here.
