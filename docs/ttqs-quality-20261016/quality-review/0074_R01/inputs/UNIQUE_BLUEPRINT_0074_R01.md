## Official 081 requirement content (four mandatory elements)

Official 081 Blueprint text: 「反應評估納入結案：分析、課程規劃與開課建議」. Per the checkbox Blueprint, an acceptable item must carry all four of the following and may not substitute empty fields for evidence:

1. **Specific source location** — a named, locatable record (form page, file, table, row, ID), not a vague reference.
2. **Substantive body or example** — real usable content, a completed worked example, or a filled instrument.
3. **Result or decision** — an outcome, conclusion, comparison, or decision record, even if it is a conditional or pending decision.
4. **Factual links to other documents** — traceable cross-document facts (case key, artifact SHA, upstream/downstream document IDs).

The genre writing manual (indicator 17a) additionally requires: showing questionnaire items/scales/options, valid-return counts, a denominator-defined response rate, identification of group/low-scoring items, instructor and learner qualitative feedback, and concrete improvements — and the closure/improvement recommendation must cite the **same returned data**.

## Unique document purpose and genre

Genre: a **course closeout report** a training administrator can actually fill in and a TTQS evaluator can inspect. It must be recognizably broader than 0073's satisfaction-and-feedback analysis. Required scope: training objective and plan-vs-delivery comparison; enrollment/attendance/completion arithmetic; instructor, materials, time, site, safety and resource accounting; a learning check with aggregates; questionnaire disposition and full item statistics; issues and limits; a closure conclusion; a comparison of next-offering design alternatives; a **conditional** opening recommendation with owner role, due event and measures; record retention; and hold/rejection criteria. It must contain one complete filled SAMPLE/SYNTHETIC example plus a separate practical replacement/checklist section for real use.

Reader: association training administrator (primary) and TTQS evaluator/internal reviewer (secondary). It is used after a course when a questionnaire has been returned and analyzed, when a learning check has been administered, and when a closure/decision record is needed.

### Distinction from DOCX 0073 (I17A-E01 / checkbox 080)
