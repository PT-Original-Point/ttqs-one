# 0069 R01 Unique Blueprint — I16-E01 / checkbox 076

## Identity and purpose
- Document ID: 0069. Official requirement: I16-E01. Official checkbox serial: 076. Keep all three distinct.
- Human-facing genre: training-process control procedure with completed implementation log, decision gates, a reusable blank record and practical exception-routing instructions.
- Unique purpose: enable a small volunteer association's training administrator to control a planned course before, during and after delivery, identify warning/stop conditions in time, preserve traceable records, and decide whether the process evidence is complete enough to release for independent review.
- Title-blind test: with the cover removed, the reader still sees a timed control sequence, role ownership, trigger thresholds, filled records, gate decisions and evidence hand-offs—not a course plan, evaluation report, KPI summary, management-review minutes, incident investigation or audit report.

## Specific full-body outline
1. Cover with prominent SAMPLE/SYNTHETIC; identity map; 30-second use card for a volunteer administrator.
2. Applicability and authority boundary; vocabulary for checkpoint, control owner, witness, deviation, warning, stop, hold, release and evidence locator.
3. One-page process map: pre-course readiness → delivery control → closeout/reconciliation → independent release; explicit handoffs to 0033 (plan only), 0066 (closeout), 0070 (qualifying exception) and 0072 (audit), each requiring exact case and source matching.
4. Responsibility matrix using functional roles only: course administrator, course lead, trainer function, venue/safety function, record custodian, independent checker. No person names or invented authority; all roles are scenario constructs.
5. Filled control-point matrix, minimum 10 distinct checks: 5 pre-course, 4 during-course, 3 post-course. Each row must include ID, timing, control objective, evidence/source locator, pass criterion, critical/noncritical status, role, actual SAMPLE observation, result, decision and next owner/deadline. Include checks of approved-plan identity, instructor/material/venue readiness, entry prerequisites, learner roster/consent boundary, attendance, schedule/method adherence, safety/environment, assessment handoff, attendance reconciliation and record retention.
6. Trigger and decision table with sample thresholds explicitly labeled local illustrative values, not official TTQS or association-approved standards. Include warning vs stop/hold, who is alerted, response deadline, evidence to retain and what requires 0070. Critical gates are not averaged away by a high completion percentage.
7. One fully populated SAMPLE/SYNTHETIC case SYN-0069-CASE-01: one-day training scenario with a fixed planned schedule, 12 enrolled, 10 attending, all pre-course critical checks complete, six timed delivery checks with one documented 8-minute deviation handled within a documented 15-minute contingency block, completion/attendance/assessment reconciliation, numerical recomputation, role-only responsibilities, exact synthetic source locators, decisions and unresolved limits. Keep the sample internally consistent and do not state this is the same case as 0033/0065/0066/0068.
8. Calculation and gate demonstration: pre-course completion 10/10=100%; attendance 10/12=83.3%; delivery checks completed 6/6=100%; on-time checkpoints 5/6=83.3%; late deviation 8 minutes against 5-minute warning and 10-minute stop thresholds; post-course required records 7/7=100%. Explain why warning is raised despite full check completion and why critical stop threshold is not met; no averaging hides risk. Include formula and numerator/denominator/period.
9. Filled disposition and follow-up register: sample result is “control record complete with timing warning; release for review only after source archive check”; no real association authorization or actual delivery is claimed. Include owner, due date, verification evidence, status and closure conditions.
10. Real-record replacement guide and explicit HOLD/rejection list for missing authority, unknown plan version, unverified participant preconditions, absent denominator, missing source, unexplained timing variance, false signatures, unrelated case IDs, or fabricated completion.
11. Blank reusable forms after the completed sample: readiness gate, live course log, post-course reconciliation, deviation/hold action and evidence index. Clearly marked blank templates; never use them instead of the filled SAMPLE case.
12. Compact cross-document fact-check matrix and SAMPLE-to-REAL replacement register with source type, exact locator, custodian role, privacy handling and reviewer check.

## Sample case data (all fictional)
- case_id `SYN-0069-CASE-01`; scenario date `2026-09-18`; schedule 09:00–12:00 and 13:00–14:00; 12 enrolled, 10 attended, attendance 10/12=83.333...%=83.3%.
- Ten pre-course controls completed 10/10; all eight critical controls pass 8/8.
- Six timed delivery checkpoints completed 6/6; five within 5 minutes, one 8 minutes late; on-time 5/6=83.333...%=83.3%. The late restart consumes 8 minutes of a separately scheduled 15-minute contingency block; instructional time is not reduced. It meets the illustrative warning trigger (>5 minutes) but not illustrative stop trigger (>10 minutes). No safety incident is part of this example. If actual safety or privacy event occurs, stop/hold and route to I16-E02/077 / DOCX 0070.
- Post-course package completeness 7/7; attendance 10/12 and assessment completion 9/10. Do not confuse 9/10 assessment completion with pass rate; no pass-rate result supplied.
- Scenario-level proposed local thresholds: readiness 100% of critical controls before start; attendance warning below 85% (no automatic cancellation); timing warning >5 min; stop >10 min or any critical safety/privacy/authority failure; post-course release requires 7/7 records. These values are illustrative only, not official/approved.
- Dates, case, data, roles, observations, thresholds, evidence locators, decisions and follow-up are invented examples and must all be clearly tagged SAMPLE/SYNTHETIC.

## Verification / rejection criteria
- Full one-to-one mapping to 076; no confusion with 077/078 or document IDs.
- Completed operational example with pre/during/post controls; blank templates are supplementary only.
- All formulas independently recompute; no threshold is misrepresented as an official standard; late minutes and contingency arithmetic reconcile.
- No fabricated real association action, authorization, participant, signature, audit, approval or outcome.
- Exact cross-document boundaries; no shared-case assertion without exact case_id and source match.
- Include evidence locators, retention/privacy steps, owner and deadline, decision/release criteria and reject/hold cases.
- Distinct from course plan, closeout, evaluation report, management-review minutes, anomaly report and audit report.
- On export: 10.5-pt minimum body font, 9.5-pt minimum table font; inspect every page at readable resolution; exact candidate SHA must bind technical, visual and independent semantic receipts before acceptance.
