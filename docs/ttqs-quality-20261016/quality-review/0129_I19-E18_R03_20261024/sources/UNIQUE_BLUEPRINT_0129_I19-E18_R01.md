# Unique Blueprint — 0129 / I19-E18 / R01

## Official purpose
Provide an operational-performance evidence document for the financial aspect of I19-E18. This candidate demonstrates the cost-control option only; it does not claim to prove market competitiveness, revenue, or complaint rate.

## Genre and audience
Evaluator-facing operational performance analysis with a usable metric dictionary, sample calculation sheet, exception analysis, evidence-replacement checklist, and hold/rejection rules. Reader: association training administrator and TTQS evaluator.

## Required sections
1. Cover and status: SAMPLE/SYNTHETIC; not a real association record or approval.
2. Requirement mapping: official checkbox and 0129 unique mapping.
3. Purpose and scope: selected operating indicator and why cost control is a valid option.
4. Metric specification: unit, numerator, denominator, exclusions, source owner, frequency, and validation.
5. Before/after sample case: two periods, eight batches, 10 cases each; reproduce from attached CSV.
6. Results table: first-pass rate, rework rate, workdays per case, assumed rework material cost.
7. Formula walk-through and reconciliation to case rows.
8. Adverse result: S-B03 first-pass count decreases 5 to 4; retain and investigate.
9. Interpretation and limitations: descriptive only; no causal training claim; small synthetic example.
10. Operational use: review cadence, variance thresholds, owner/action/deadline fields as blank/sample placeholders.
11. Evidence replacement register and provenance instructions.
12. Hold/rejection criteria and sign-off placeholders; no invented approvals.

## Quantitative invariants
- 80 cases in each period.
- Pre: 55/80 first-pass, 25/80 reworked, 400 aggregate workdays, NT$200 assumed sample rework material cost.
- Post: 66/80 first-pass, 14/80 reworked, 265 aggregate workdays, NT$112 assumed sample rework material cost.
- First-pass change +13.75 percentage points; mean workdays change -33.75%; assumed rework material cost change -44%.
- Every numeric statement must reconcile to the case-level CSV and deterministic QA receipt.

## Hold/reject rules
Hold REAL use if period definitions, source systems, population, sample counts, unit-cost basis, timestamps, responsible reviewer, or original records cannot be verified. Reject if synthetic values are presented as association facts, a causal training effect is asserted without design, the adverse S-B03 result is hidden, or full financial/market performance is claimed from this limited cost demonstration.
