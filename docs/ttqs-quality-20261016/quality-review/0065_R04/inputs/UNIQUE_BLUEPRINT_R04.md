- `doc_id=0065`; `requirement_id=I15-E01`; official checkbox `071`; title `訓練後定期綜合檢討／管理審查會議紀錄（單一合成案例）`.
- Source hashes and authority are listed in `SOURCE_FACTS_AND_MAPPING_PUBLIC.md`. Official page is page 5 of the evaluator evidence table.

## Unique purpose and title-blind test

After training and the course-closing report exist, enable a small volunteer association’s course-planning/administration functions to review what happened, compare plan/targets with results, identify what the evidence can and cannot support, record differing viewpoints, formulate traceable proposed actions, name functional owners and dates, and define the next effectiveness check. If the title/header is hidden, a reader must recognize a review meeting because the core is agenda → evidence-led discussion → meeting conclusion → responsible follow-up, rather than a course plan or KPI dashboard.

A filled pre-meeting evidence dashboard with numerator, denominator, period/date, source, plan or explicitly illustrative sample threshold, result, gap in percentage points/minutes/NT dollars, limitation and decision question. Include attendance/authority, agenda, source packet, discussion, decision, owner/deadline, evidence location, approval, distribution and retention fields.
10. HOLD/rejection conditions: missing official source/page; absent denominator/source/period; inconsistent case facts; unsupported causality; sample values presented as real; invented identities/signatures/committee approval; stale/unverified action completion; budget/receipt without originals; confusion with 0066/0067/0068/0039.

- Assessment coverage 9/10 = 90.0%; illustrative threshold 90%; gap 0.0 pp.
