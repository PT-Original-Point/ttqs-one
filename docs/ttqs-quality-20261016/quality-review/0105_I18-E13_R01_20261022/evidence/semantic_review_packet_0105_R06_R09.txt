# UNIQUE BLUEPRINT 0105 / I18-E13 / R01

## 1. Identity, official mapping and genre

- `project_id`: TTQS_ONE
- `doc_id`: `0105` (document ID; not an official checkbox serial)
- `official_requirement_id`: `I18-E13` (R04 142 crosswalk)
- Official source: training-institution evidence table, printed page 7, I18-L3, 3-point common evidence example: `問卷調查及課後行動計畫評估表等顯示目標客戶及學員認為訓練有績效改善的效果。`

- Genre: operational questionnaire and post-training action-plan evaluation package, including administration instructions, response instrument, scoring worksheet, follow-up form, worked synthetic example, decision/hold thresholds and synthetic replacement register.
- Primary readers: small association training staff, target customers/learners completing the survey, and TTQS reviewers checking the evidence chain.

## 2. The job this document must do

Enable an association administrator to: define a specific expected work-performance change before training; administer a brief, understandable survey to learners and target customers; protect privacy; summarize valid responses with transparent denominators; create a post-course action plan with owners, due dates and evidence sources; conduct a timed follow-up; distinguish perceived effects from verified performance; and decide whether the evidence supports a limited descriptive statement, requires HOLD, or can be escalated for human review.

The form does not itself establish that training caused a change or that the association achieved an outcome. The official phrase is a common evidence example under the 3-point section, not a score promise.

## 3. Mandatory content architecture

1. Cover with prominent `SAMPLE / SYNTHETIC` notice, exact requirement, purpose, reader, timing, and 30-second use instructions.
2. One-page definitions: target customer vs learner; expected performance; self-report vs objective record; baseline; target; follow-up; valid response; nonresponse; evidence status; causal limitation.
3. Roles/authority/privacy procedure: who may invite, collect, de-identify, analyze, approve access and store records; use role labels, minimum necessary data, consent and opt-out; no invented delegation.
4. Administration SOP with input, steps, timing, denominator rules, duplicate handling, nonresponse, missing/NA values, output and exception paths.
5. Form A — survey cover/control record: case/course ID, target group, invitation count, collection window, consent scope, version, custodial role, response channel and de-identification key location.
6. Form B — complete learner questionnaire and branching notes; at least 12 substantive items with a five-point scale (`1 strongly disagree` to `5 strongly agree`) plus `N/A / unable to judge`, clear instructions and at least two open-text prompts. Ask separately about learning/application, task quality or time, perceived organizational/service performance, observed evidence, other contributing changes, and action follow-through. Do not force an opinion.
7. Form C — target-customer questionnaire or clearly branched equivalent, phrased so a customer can judge observed service/work change without being asked to rate internal training quality they did not observe.
8. Form D — data-entry and scoring sheet: invitation/response/valid denominators; positive share; item-level distribution; subgroup counts; missing/NA treatment; no unsupported composite score. Each formula must show numerator, denominator and rounding.
9. Form E — post-training action plan: expected result, baseline and source, measurable target, actions, owner role, due dates, resources, barriers, check method, follow-up date, outcome source, evidence locator, status and corrective step.
10. Form F — follow-up evaluation: planned vs actual action, source-check state, baseline/target/current value, deviation, alternative explanations, privacy status, reviewer role and disposition (`OPEN`, `HOLD`, `CLOSED_FOR_SAMPLE`, `ESCALATE_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW`).
11. Worked synthetic case using stable case ID `SYN-0105-CASE-01`. State that the class, respondents and all values are invented. Complete invitation and response counts, subgroup breakdown, item distribution and action-plan follow-up with consistent arithmetic. Any apparent work-performance change remains sample-only and noncausal.
12. Results interpretation: descriptive statements only; discuss small N, self-selection, nonresponse, recall/social-desirability bias, missing objective sources, other causes and limits of generalization. Provide “what may be said / what must not be said” examples.
13. Decision/hold table for too few responses, missing consent, duplicated response, no baseline, no follow-up, contradictory data, unsupported financial claim, unverified source, privacy concern, or causal overclaim.
14. Replacement register with one synthetic fact per row: `fact_id`, `case_id`, document ID, section/form/cell, synthetic value, real source needed, responsible role, state, and replacement instructions.
15. Filing, retention and access log fields; retention period is `set from current law/consent/policy`, not invented. Include version/change log and quick final completeness checklist.
